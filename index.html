ABOUT ME

I'm a multimedia marketer who genuinely enjoys building things from the ground up, whether that's a content pipeline, a brand's social presence, or a video edit that needs to hit right on the first cut. Over the past few years I've run production solo, led a team through a national competition, and picked up new tools fast enough that not knowing something yet has never really slowed me down.
